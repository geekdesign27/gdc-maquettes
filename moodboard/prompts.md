## Brief de style (premier message, avec les deux références jointes)

> I am building the art direction for "WORKSITE", a stylized isometric construction management game. I attach two style references: REF_ENV (construction site: take its shapes, colors and composition) and REF_CHR (characters: take the faces, hair and skin). I will ask you for several reference images, one per message. Apply this style to ALL of them:
> - "Soft 3D" render: gentle gradient shading, subtle satin highlights, rounded bevelled edges that catch the light, large flat faces. NOT flat vector like REF_ENV. No photo textures, no rust, no grime, no text, no logos.
> - Orthographic isometric camera (45° rotation, 35° elevation), square image.
> - One soft key light from the upper left: top faces show the true palette color, left faces about 85 % of it, right faces about 65 %, soft contact shadows.
> - Warm off-white background #F8F8F6.
> - Palette: concrete #D9DDE1, off-white #F1EFEB, graphite #303841, construction orange #F5A000, coral brick #EF655F (structures under construction only), tech blue #4D9DBD, sage green #83A78F, asphalt #6C6674 with white dashes, wood #D2A273, glass #9CC3D5.
> - Every place sits on a thick earth-colored diorama base slab (#BF8B65) with a visible edge, like an architect's model.
> - A clean, active, organised Swiss (Romandie) construction site. Warm and professional, not toy-like.
> Reply "ready" and wait for my first request.
